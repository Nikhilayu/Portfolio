02How I work

Curiosity first, craft always. A simple rhythm that leaves room for surprise.

Listen

I start by asking a lot of questions. The best ideas usually hide in the answers you didn't expect.

Wander

Sketches, references, bad ideas on purpose. This is where the project finds its personality.

Make

Shaping the strongest direction with care, from the big gesture down to the smallest detail.

Polish

Testing it in the real world, refining, and handing over something that's ready to live.

Hello, I'm Nikhil.

I'm a creative who lives in the space between art and purpose. I've worked with small studios, independent brands, and plenty of my own experiments, always chasing the moment an idea clicks into shape.

I believe good work should be generous: clear enough to understand, strange enough to remember. When I'm not designing, I'm sketching, collecting typefaces, or wandering around with a camera.
